FlexRoute vs Routerra

Checked against each company's own pages on 30 October 2026

The short version

  • Routerra's free plan is bigger: 20 stops per route and 5 optimizations a day (30 a month), with no credit card. FlexRoute gives 5 optimized routes in total, with the first 15 stops of each.
  • FlexRoute costs less once you pay: $9.99 a month or $79 a year, against Routerra Pro at $14.99 a month, or $9.99 a month billed annually (about $120 a year).
  • Routerra has apps for iPhone and Android as well as the web. FlexRoute has an Android app and works in any phone browser, but has no iPhone app yet.
  • Routerra Pro adds traffic-aware optimization and up to 200 stops per route. FlexRoute's driving times come from road data, not live traffic.

Where Routerra is the better choice

  • You use an iPhone and want a native app.
  • Your routes are 20 stops or fewer. The free plan is generous and renews every day.
  • You need paid features on only one or two days a month. A $5 day pass is cheaper than any monthly plan.
  • You want traffic-aware optimization.
  • You plan very large routes, up to 200 stops.

Where FlexRoute is the better choice

  • You pay for a plan. FlexRoute is $5 a month cheaper, and about $41 a year cheaper, than Routerra Pro.
  • You want the shift guided stop by stop, with the package count for each stop in front of you.
  • You want to pay once for a month, without a subscription.
  • You work in French, German, Spanish or Portuguese.
  • You want help spotting a stop the app may have put on the wrong street. FlexRoute checks each pin against the street in its address, and marks the ones it could not confirm.

Try it before you decide

FlexRoute lets you optimize 5 real routes without paying, so you can see the saving on your own streets. If Routerra's free plan covers your routes, or you need an iPhone app, it is a sound choice.
