FlexRoute vs FixMyRoute

Checked against each company's own pages on 30 October 2026

The short version

  • FixMyRoute has the bigger free plan. It is free up to 27 stops per route, with no account. Bigger routes cost $0.99 each, and there is no subscription.
  • FlexRoute's free plan is smaller: 5 optimized routes in total, with the first 15 stops of each. After that it is $9.99 a month, $79 a year, or $9.99 once for a 30-day pass.
  • If you rarely go past 27 stops, FixMyRoute is probably the cheaper choice. If most of your routes are bigger and you work most days, a flat plan costs less.
  • What FlexRoute adds is Shift Mode (one stop at a time), package counts on every stop, and a check that each pin sits on the street in its address.

Where FixMyRoute is the better choice

  • Most of your routes have 27 stops or fewer. It is free, with no account.
  • You only occasionally have a big route. $0.99 for that one is cheaper than any subscription.
  • You would simply rather not subscribe to anything.

Where FlexRoute is the better choice

  • You do big routes most days. Paying per route adds up (see the arithmetic below).
  • You want the shift guided stop by stop, with the package count for each stop in front of you.
  • You want to pay once for a month, without a subscription, for example during peak season.
  • You work in French, German, Spanish or Portuguese.
  • You want help spotting a stop the app may have put on the wrong street. FlexRoute checks each pin against the street in its address, and marks the ones it could not confirm.

The price arithmetic

Take FixMyRoute at $0.99 per larger route. Ten routes over 27 stops cost $9.90 in a month, which is just under FlexRoute's $9.99. The eleventh costs $10.89 in total, so from there a flat $9.99 is cheaper. Routes of 27 stops or fewer are free on FixMyRoute, so this only counts your big ones. If you have few of them, FixMyRoute wins on price.
