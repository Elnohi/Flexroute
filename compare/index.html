How FlexRoute compares

Checked against each company's own pages on 30 October 2026

Several route planners can read a Flex itinerary from a screenshot. They differ mainly in what is free, what paying costs, and which phones they run on. We have compared FlexRoute with two of them, using each company's own pages, and we say where the other one is the better choice.

FlexRoute vs FixMyRouteFixMyRoute is free up to 27 stops and charges $0.99 for bigger routes, with no subscription. Better if most of your routes are small. FlexRoute vs RouterraRouterra is free for 20 stops a day and has an iPhone app. FlexRoute costs less once you pay, and adds Shift Mode.

How to choose

  • Small routes, or only now and then: start with a free plan. FixMyRoute (27 stops) and Routerra (20 stops, 5 a day) both give more free use than FlexRoute does.
  • Big routes most days: a flat plan usually costs less than paying per route. FlexRoute is $9.99 a month or $79 a year.
  • On an iPhone and want a native app: Routerra has one. FlexRoute works in the iPhone browser but has no App Store app yet.
  • Want the shift guided one stop at a time: that is FlexRoute's Shift Mode.

Whatever you pick, check any route before you drive it. No planner can know about a closed road or a locked gate.
