Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Last updated 30 October 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an account or a password to use FlexRoute, and it never asks for your delivery-platform login.
  • Your stops, depot and home address live in your phone's local storage — not on our servers.
  • Addresses are sent to mapping services so they can be turned into map pins and a route. That's what makes the app work.
  • Screenshots you scan are read for text and then discarded. We don't keep them.
  • After your first free route the app asks for your email. We use it, with a device identifier, only to count your free routes. If you subscribe, your email is also used to sign you in and link your purchase. Card details go straight to Stripe — we never see them.
  • We use Google Analytics to see which features get used. It never receives your addresses, screenshots or email.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't run ads.

The rest of this page explains each of those points precisely, including exactly which companies receive which data and how long each kind of record is kept.

What this version adds. Earlier versions left out some things we do. This one spells them out: the email address and device identifier used to count free routes (section 4), the security log (section 4), Google Analytics and its cookies (section 6), the end-of-shift survey (section 3), the emails we send about your plan (section 5), and a stated retention period for every kind of record (section 7).

1. Who we are

FlexRoute is an independent route-optimisation tool operated by a single developer. You can reach us at flexrouteapp@gmail.com or through our contact page.

FlexRoute is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Amazon.com, Inc. or any of its subsidiaries. We are not affiliated with any delivery platform.

2. What FlexRoute stores on your device

Most of what you put into FlexRoute never leaves your phone. The following are saved in your browser's local storage so the app still works the next time you open it:

  • Your current list of stops and the optimised order
  • Your depot / warehouse start address
  • Your home address, if you set one as an end point
  • Preferences: region, distance units, navigation app, text size, map zoom
  • Shift Mode progress — which stops you've completed
  • An optional Google Maps API key, if you choose to add your own

We cannot read any of this remotely. You can erase all of it at any time using Settings → Clear app data, or by clearing site data for FlexRoute in your browser or Android app settings. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

About scanning

Depending on your device, scanning happens either entirely on your phone or on our server. When it happens on the server, the image is sent to Google Cloud Vision, the text is extracted, and the image is discarded when the request finishes. FlexRoute does not save your screenshots, and does not store them in any database.

Scanned screenshots typically contain delivery addresses and package numbers. Please avoid uploading images containing information you don't want processed — you can always add stops by typing them instead.

About location

If you tap Use GPS to detect location, your browser asks for permission and FlexRoute uses those coordinates once to fill in your starting point. We don't track you in the background, we don't log your movements during a shift, and we don't record where you drive. Declining the permission only means you type your depot address instead.

About addresses and privacy

To be direct with you: delivery addresses and your home address are personal information. FlexRoute is built so that this data stays on your device wherever possible, but geocoding and routing genuinely require sending addresses to the mapping services listed above. Any tool that turns an address into a route must do this. What we can promise is that we don't store them server-side, don't attach them to your identity, and don't sell or share them for advertising.

4. Accounts, email and free-route tracking

FlexRoute has no accounts and no passwords. There are two situations in which we hold an email address.

Free routes

FlexRoute includes a limited number of free optimised routes. After your first one, the app asks for your email address. We store it on our server with how many free routes you have used and the date of the last one. The address isn't verified, and we don't email you because of it. Its only purpose is to count your free routes.

When you enter your email, the app also sends a device identifier: a short code calculated from your browser's user-agent string, language, screen size and colour depth, time zone, number of processor cores and platform, and from how your browser draws a small hidden test image (sometimes called a canvas fingerprint). We use it for one thing: counting free routes per device, so that clearing your browser data and typing a different email doesn't hand out unlimited free routes. We don't use it for advertising or to follow you between sites or apps, and the code can't be turned back into the settings it was made from. Because it comes from device settings, two people with identical phones and settings can occasionally end up sharing a count. If that happens to you, email us and we'll fix it.

Subscribing

If you subscribe or buy a pass, we ask for an email address and send you a six-digit sign-in code. We store that email, the code (for 10 minutes) and a sign-in session (for 180 days) so we can confirm it's you and check whether your plan is active.

When you sign in or start a checkout, we also write a short security log entry: the time, your IP address, a shortened hash of your browser's user-agent string, and your email address. We use it to spot abuse, such as one address requesting codes for many different emails.

5. Payments and the emails we send

Plans and passes are handled by Stripe. Card numbers are entered on Stripe's own checkout page and are never transmitted to or stored by FlexRoute. Stripe keeps your payment record; on our side we keep only your email and the records described in section 7.

We don't send marketing email, newsletters or promotions. The only emails we send are about your own plan:

  • the six-digit sign-in code;
  • about ten days after you subscribe, a short check-in asking how FlexRoute is working for you (it may include a link to leave a review);
  • after you cancel a monthly or yearly plan, a short note asking what led you to cancel;
  • for a 30-day pass, a reminder about three days before it ends, with a link to buy another.

Every email except the sign-in code has a one-click unsubscribe link, and we keep a list of addresses that have unsubscribed so we never email them again. They are sent through Resend. Replies come to our inbox, and we read them.

6. Analytics and cookies

FlexRoute uses Google Analytics 4 on flexrouteapp.com and inside the app, to learn which features are used and what breaks.

  • What it records: which screens are opened and which features are used (for example “route optimised” or “Shift Mode started”), counts such as how many stops a route had, your app version and language, and basic device, browser and approximate location (country and region) details that Google works out from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses.
  • What it never receives: your addresses, screenshots, stop details or email address.
  • Cookies: Google sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) to tell visits apart. They last up to two years.
  • Who runs it: Google, acting for us under its data processing terms. We don't use it for advertising or remarketing.

You can opt out with Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on, by blocking cookies or analytics in your browser, or with a content blocker. FlexRoute works the same either way.

7. How long we keep things

Records we hold are deleted automatically once they pass the periods below.

  • On-device data — until you clear it or uninstall. We have no copy.
  • Screenshots — not retained. Discarded once text is extracted.
  • Addresses sent for geocoding — not retained by us. Third-party providers keep their own logs under their own policies.
  • Sign-in codes — valid for 10 minutes and deleted when used; any left unused are removed a day after they expire.
  • Sign-in sessions — 180 days, then deleted.
  • Free-route records (your email, device identifier, route count and last-used date) — deleted 24 months after you last used a free route.
  • Security log (IP address, browser hash, email) — deleted after 180 days.
  • Survey answers — deleted after 12 months.
  • Records that an email was sent to you (used so we never send the same email twice) — deleted after 24 months.
  • Unsubscribe list — kept until you ask us to remove an address, because deleting it would mean emailing people who asked us not to.
  • Payments and subscriptions — held by Stripe for as long as tax and accounting law requires. We don't keep card details.
  • Analytics — held by Google Analytics for no more than 14 months.
  • Server logs — kept briefly by Netlify for security and diagnostics.

8. Your rights

Because most of your data is on your own device, the fastest way to exercise most rights is Settings → Clear app data, which is immediate and complete.

For anything we hold server-side — your email, free-route record, device identifier, sign-in records, security log entries and survey answers — you can ask us to show it to you, correct it, delete it, or send you a copy. Email flexrouteapp@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, the GDPR gives you these rights plus the right to object to processing and to complain to your national data protection authority. Our lawful bases are: performance of a contract, for delivering the service you asked for; legitimate interests, for security, for preventing repeated free-route claims, and for understanding how the app is used (you can opt out of analytics, as section 6 explains); and consent, for optional device location.

If you're in California, the CCPA gives you rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, and we never have.

9. Children

FlexRoute is a tool for working delivery drivers and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

Traffic to FlexRoute is encrypted over HTTPS. Payments are isolated at Stripe. Our backend functions reject requests that don't originate from our own domain, which limits third parties from running up our billed services in your name.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, please tell us at flexrouteapp@gmail.com before disclosing it publicly — we'll respond quickly and credit you if you'd like.

11. International transfers

Our providers — Netlify, Stripe, Google, Resend, and the mapping services — operate servers in several countries, including the United States. Using FlexRoute means your data may be processed outside your home country under those providers' own safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how FlexRoute handles data, we'll update this page and move the "last updated" date at the top. For changes that meaningfully affect your privacy, we'll show a notice in the app. Continuing to use FlexRoute after a change means you accept the updated policy.
