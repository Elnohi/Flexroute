Welcome to FlexRoute 👋

Get the shortest route for your Amazon Flex shift in 3 quick steps.
1
📸 Screenshot your itinerary
Open the Amazon Flex app and take a screenshot of your stop list (the address list view).
2
📤 Upload it to FlexRoute
Tap the upload button below. FlexRoute reads the addresses automatically — no typing needed.
3
🚀 Get your optimized route
Tap “Optimize Route” to get the shortest path. Then open it in Google Maps or Waze with one tap.
⚡ No connection — route still works, geocoding needs internet
